## What Toogo should take
1. **One-line test.** Before the store subtitle, landing and first screen, check a stranger can say what Toogo is after one glance, in Hebrew and English (toogo-store-listing, product.md).
2. **Short-video text rules.** Six words, 1.2 s hold, Hebrew checked separately; one ask per caption (toogo-short-video).
3. **Three video types by viewer stage:** curious, understand, decide (toogo-short-video).
4. **Skeleton screens and instant feedback** on Home, Discover and the trip list (toogo-first-run, toogo-premium-feel).
5. **Landing or tester waitlist:** a short swipe-and-match recording plus one field, then a "what happens next" message (toogo-first-run, toogo-indie-launch).
6. **Motion personality** for splash and match pop is the owner's call (toogo-premium-feel).
- **Rules check:** no fake urgency or scarcity (rule 7); credits and money model not touched (not decided); a place enters a trip only by swipe and match (any demo video must show that flow, not a shortcut); every UI wording change needs English (rule 1). Their paid-service funnel is not relevant.
