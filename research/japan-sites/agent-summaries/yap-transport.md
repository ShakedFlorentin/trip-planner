# yapanit.com transport + events: summary

Pages: 43 fetched, all HTTP 200 (raw HTML in raw/, trimmed article text in text/). Places: 19 (4 flagged weak). Tips: 51.

## Most useful
- JR Pass (Oct 2023 price rise to 50,000/80,000/100,000 yen), break-even rule of thumb, regional passes (Kansai-Hiroshima, Hakone, Nikko, Kansai Wide), exchange-office hours.
- Airport to city for Narita, Haneda and Kansai (times, yen, last trains).
- Festival calendar with dates and access: Otsu, Jidai, Kurama, Daimonji, Chichibu, Kanamara, Sumida/Edogawa/Miyajima fireworks, Obon, Meguro sakura.
- Highway bus operators and prices, car rental rules (international licence, ETC, drop-off fees).
- Tokyo transit: Yamanote, Suica, day passes.

## Outdated or doubtful
- JR Pass pages are written Oct 2023 or earlier; prices changed again since (not verified here).
- Undated and probably old: Hakone Freepass fares, Seishun 18 (11,850 yen), Kansai Wide Pass, Narita fares, highway-bus fares, Kansai One Day Pass 2,000 yen, Nikko pass.
- Hello Kitty Shinkansen page says it ran only to Oct 2018.
- Festival years on pages range 2017-2024, so the recurring rule (e.g. last Saturday of July) is more reliable than the stated date. Chichibu text is 2017.
- Takayama dates (9-10 Apr, 14-15 Oct) have no year.
- Page /פסטיבל-הזיקוקין-באנושימה-פוג-יסאווה (Enoshima/Fujisawa fireworks) contains the Miyajima text; no real Enoshima data.
- Tokyo Marathon page is mostly 2020 trivia; Obon page has no place list.

## Gaps
- Lectures/guided tours pages are the author's own business events (Nov 2026 online lecture, tours 2023-24): kept as one tip only.
- Hakone/Nikko hotels, shinkansen fare tables, timetable are images not in text; Tokyo train map and itinerary tables are images.
- Affiliate links (jrpass.com, Klook, Tocoo) and a coupon code were ignored on purpose.
- Few coordinates; only Kurama has a maps link.
