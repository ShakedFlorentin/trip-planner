# ptitim.com summary

Counts: places.jsonl 135 rows, tips.jsonl 74 rows, 50 candidate pages fetched (raw/), 38 skipped as recipes, WordPress API searched for 11 terms (no new Japan travel posts; only blogday2011 and georgia surfaced, both not Japan).

Most useful: /tokyoguide/ (updated July 2026, covers all Tokyo area descriptions, restaurant "gold list" with Google Maps links, eating/ordering/payment etiquette, dietary restriction notes, shopping lists, Kyoto section). The author's full Tokyo map is an external Google My Maps (mid=1I0o12hoecmBorcEsinQqw4nhTDG7adU) and is NOT on the page, so only the roughly 50 gold-list/named places are captured. Next: /japan2014/ (Hokkaido and Tohoku trip, names only, details are on the external blog shinkansening.blogspot.co.il), the noodle guide, pantry guide, miso, umami, dashi, matcha, rice, ramen (food-knowledge tips).

Gaps:
- /japan2016/ and /אני-ביפן/ contain only a short blurb pointing to external blogs (memoriesofgaijin.blogspot.com, streetfoodfighter.blogspot.com); those blogs are not scraped here (they contain a lot of place detail: e.g. Oiwake Dango, Kasagiya tea house, Tamawarai soba restaurant).
- No Osaka, Nara, Hiroshima, Nikko, Hakone content apart from a Kyoto summary; no addresses, hours, or prices for most places (only Google Maps short links).
- No kosher-certified places; guide only explains how to avoid pork/fish/gluten. No baby/stroller info (author explicitly says he does not cover kids).
- Many Hebrew-language marked-up links to Google Maps are goo.gl short links and may be stale.

Outdated/age notes: Tokyo guide is updated through July 2026 (Shinjuku Gyoen 500 yen card-only, IC card details as of July 2026). Hokkaido/Tohoku content from 2014; Yanaka/Gundam statements from 2022-2023 (Gundam in Odaiba was due to move; the statue date was not verified, so it was not added as a place). Some places may have closed (author warns many restaurants closed after covid). Uogashi Nihon-Ichi: author says quality dropped on last visit.

Notes: the Tokyo guide comments section was skimmed; one comment confirms Kitchen Nankai (curry) and Sakaezushi (sushi) as authentic and good value. Some chains and places have no maps links because the guide gave none.
