# Marketing craft (Toogo)

Short rules taught by the teacher agent from creators' reels, each with a why and its source. `toogo-rules` wins over anything here (no fake urgency, no squeezing, money model not decided). Narrow topics also live in `toogo-short-video`, `toogo-store-listing`, `toogo-growth-loops`, `toogo-indie-launch`, `toogo-credits-offer`. All numbers are creators' claims; the evidence for most of this is thin (small accounts, articles). Hebrew first, English second.

## Positioning

- **One line: who it is for and what it does,** readable in one glance, in both languages. Test the subtitle and first screenshot caption on a stranger. @theglidestudio https://www.instagram.com/theglidestudio/reel/Dfw1tros6ZG/
- **The brand is the whole folder,** not the logo: icon, name, subtitle, screenshots, preview video, share card, reviewer notes. Check side by side. @luca_radez https://www.instagram.com/luca_radez/reel/DfXubRAAIgw/
- **A name with a one-sentence story** in Hebrew and English; no invented claims. https://www.instagram.com/luca_radez/p/DfSectlDDFi/
- **One idea drives the brand;** accent used like light. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfKL3ANARku/
- **Motion is part of the brand:** pick one personality. https://www.instagram.com/theglidestudio/reel/Dfzkwu3hSj5/

## Reels and hooks

Full formats: `toogo-short-video`.
- **Hook = another person + a conflict + they changed their mind** ("she wanted the beach, I wanted museums"). Claimed 147K-234K views against 0.9K-2.7K for feature hooks (Oliver Henry, article).
- **Blunt first line, then steps over real screens** (@danh.app, best reels 3.6K and 3.5K views on 304 followers): honest, no insulting viewers, pay off the hook.
- **Text on video:** six words max per screen, large, held about 1.2 s; readable twice; check Hebrew separately. https://www.instagram.com/theglidestudio/reel/DfxpIbfP-SB/
- **Pick the format by viewer stage:** curious (launch reel), understand (explainer), decide (real-screen demo). One of each. https://www.instagram.com/theglidestudio/p/DfxKTPhjkba/
- **A demo is not a screen recording:** crop to one flow, phone-big, a finger leads; show swipe and match, never a shortcut. https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfv1BkEO_YT/
- **Build it in front of them:** one day plan assembled card by card, each by a real swipe and match, from a fixture trip. https://www.instagram.com/luca_radez/reel/Dfes-rkgUkD/
- **One component, one line, one question;** a numbered series with true dates only. @aniakubow https://www.instagram.com/aniakubow/reel/DfUzxobxFfa/
- **Put the full rule list in the caption;** it is what gets saved. Give value, never gate it behind "comment a word". @charles.unemployed
- **One ask per caption;** the question or save line closes it.
- **Repost a clip with a new caption or cover** rather than delete it. @danh.app
- **Photo slideshow:** 6 portrait slides, hook on the first, only photos that pass rule 5. TikTok first; expect a flat start (one founder waited about 6 months).
- **Voice-over:** free local TTS made once on the Mac (not in the app); check Hebrew quality; never clone a real voice. @itstundeanthony, see `toogo-short-video`.
- **Not adopted:** comment-for-DM funnels, countdowns, paid influencer deals before organic works, phone farms.

## Store listing

Details in `toogo-store-listing`.
- **App name is a search term** ("Toogo - Japan Trip Planner"); a Hebrew listing for Israel plus English; per-country keywords from Apple Search Ads popularity. @ayoships reels 14, 17.
- **5 real screenshots,** headline each, one accent on the one thing, Hebrew set mirrored; nothing not yet live (rule 6).
- **Study two competitor listings** before writing ours. https://www.instagram.com/danh.app/reel/DfvTxL5ScLH/
- **In-app events** for a dated honest moment only, after the trip, no streak or "limited time" framing.
- **Free landing pages that answer searches** ("where to stay in Kyoto") from cached data, plus listing on AlternativeTo and SaaSHub. @ayoships. Idea for the owner.

## Launch

Details in `toogo-indie-launch`, `toogo-app-review`.
- **Fix App Review basics first:** privacy and terms, numbered reviewer notes, demo account with a ready trip, delete account, AI disclosure.
- **Launch after approval, by hand,** after the Japan trip (rule 6); have the first video series ready, not rushed. December is crowded.
- **Say what happens next** after a tester applies; a small capacity may be stated once, plainly, if true.

## Growth loops

Details in `toogo-growth-loops`.
- **A read-only shared trip link that opens without sign-up,** looks great, loads fast, has one "plan your own" button and a good preview image.
- **Share card for Stories** ("trip wrapped"): image plus the system share sheet; free. https://www.instagram.com/danh.app/reel/DdELXgxSBNf/
- **Quiet milestones** (first match, 10 places) with no streaks or loss messages.
- **Feedback door:** private report now; a public votes board needs the owner's privacy decision.
- **Rating prompt:** Apple's own, after a win, not on first visit.
- **If a partner (affiliate hotel) is ever shown, label it as a partnership.** @aniakubow sponsored posts.

## Pricing ideas (options, not decisions)

See `toogo-credits-offer`. Show the finished plan, then the price; show exactly what is charged and when; offer at the moment of need; no repeat offer within a week; same price for everyone; free swipe and match stay free; never different prices by profile.

## Frames read

Seen in video frames, 2026-11-16.
- **Reel shape that works for craft accounts**: the finished result first, then numbered steps on screen (the 10-hacks list highlights the current item with a running timer; the Glide studies use 'Before / Same screen, designed' split), ending on one line (Save it / Send it to your designer). For Toogo reels: show the swipe-and-match flow as the 'after'. No DM funnels. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfxOhZmyLXz/ and https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfv1BkEO_YT/
- **Brand concept reel** (@luca_radez, Alba airline): name, one line ('Fly at first light'), then applications (stationery, apps) in a numbered series ('Brand Roulette Ep. 01'). A series title makes each reel a repeatable format. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfKL3ANARku/ (mostly visual; no text lesson beyond this)
- **Pricing page detail**: a monthly/yearly toggle that rolls the price digits and shows 'billed once a year' under the number (see frontend). The wording is clear, not pushy: fits rule 7. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdtlmICKd8p/
