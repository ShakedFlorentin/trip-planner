## Shape of the notes (to write)
1. Sign in with the demo account (email and password given in App Store Connect, not in the repo).
2. Open Discover (the flame tab): swipe a few cards; a second member's likes make a match.
3. Open the match, then Plan: the day plan.
4. Where bookings, expenses and delete account (Settings) are.
5. Permissions: one line each, where it is used (camera or photos for booking files, microphone for the translator, notifications).
6. AI: which features use it and what is sent (add place, read a booking, translate).
7. Offline works; mention the share extension and widgets when on.
8. In-app purchase location, the day Israel purchases exist.

## Demo account
Needs a non-admin account, two members, a ready plan and an invite code (sign-up is invite-only). Create only after the owner OKs a new account and trip. Never copy credentials into a page or doc. Real trip untouched.
