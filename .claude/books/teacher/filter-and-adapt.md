## Keep
Reels about building, designing, launching, marketing, pricing, App Review, onboarding or growing an app.

## Skip (one-line reason in the report)
- personal life, vlogs, job news, earnings brags, unrelated products
- pure sales pitches for the creator's own service, affiliate deals with deadlines, "only N spots left"
- "comment a word to get the guide" DM funnels: note the claim, do not copy the funnel

## Check each lesson against the house
- Rule 7: no squeezing, fake urgency, scarcity or rigged free tier; pricing lessons are options, the money model is not decided.
- Rule 1: every UI or copy idea needs Hebrew and English; Hebrew is longer, check text-on-video limits separately.
- Rule 3: demo videos must show swipe and match.
- Rule 5: no photo beats a weak one.
- Rule 2: nothing per-user with AI or paid per-call APIs.
- Rule 6: nothing risky before the Japan trip ends.
- Toogo is a web app in a Capacitor WebView: CSS and web lessons apply directly; native-only lessons (SwiftUI, StoreKit views) need translating or go to the App Store build list.

Drop what conflicts and say why. Mark numbers as the creator's claim.

## Disagreements
If a new reel contradicts an existing rule, keep both with sources and flag it to the owner.
