## What it says (8 points)
1. Data sits in one Supabase database; trips are separated and only joined members see them.
2. The owner can technically read everything through the dashboard and promises not to open or share it except to fix a reported fault.
3. Booking files, links and names go to Claude (Anthropic), including web search; manual entry is possible.
4. Do not enter card numbers, passwords, full passport numbers or ID documents.
5. A place you add goes to the shared library (name, area, description, address; no notes or votes).
6. Trial stage: errors possible, no uptime promise, may not stay free, keep your own copy.
7. Delete account and trip on request, within reasonable time.
8. Honest feedback; reports are visible to your trip's members and the owner.

## Gaps vs the other drafts
- It says passport numbers should not be uploaded, but the privacy draft says booking files can include them: align the two.
- Deletion on request, by hand, until in-app delete is live.
- Plural "we" tone in the feedback line; the rest is third person "the owner".
- Add the affiliate sentence when partner links go live (see [affiliate-disclosure](affiliate-disclosure.md)).
- Not an English version: write one if EN testers join.
