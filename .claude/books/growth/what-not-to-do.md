title: What not to do (growth rules)
chapter: rules
---
From `toogo-rules`, `toogo-short-video`, `toogo-growth-loops`, `toogo-credits-offer`.

- No fake urgency, countdowns, "last spots", streaks or loss messages (rule 7). A real small capacity may be said once, plainly.
- No nagging: no sign-up sheet every few swipes, no repeated offers. A closed offer stays quiet for a week.
- No rigged free tier: swipe, match and seeing the plan stay free.
- No place straight into a trip from a share, import or importer-style "turn reels into a trip" (rule 3).
- No weak cards or weak photos in videos, screenshots or imports (rules 4, 5).
- Never show or promise where-to-stay or anything not live (rule 6).
- Rating prompt: Apple's own only, after a win, never on the first visit, never rewarded.
- Outside the US storefront no button, link or hint of paying on the web (Apple 3.1.1).
- No phone farms, comment bots, fake accounts, "comment X to get the guide" funnels, paid influencer deals before organic works.
- No session replay or new tracking without the owner's privacy decision. Never track typed text.
- No real account in tests or screenshots; made-up data only (rule 11).
- No posting or messaging on the owner's behalf.
- No claim or proof lines on the landing or shared page (owner: "too much AI"); short copy, app screens, one button.
- No "comment a word" gates, no blind-box scarcity, no email list (owner decision), no tracking by Wi-Fi, battery or device fingerprints.
- If a partner hotel link ever shows, label it as a partnership.
- Do not claim numbers from creators as results: label them their claim.
- Do not tune a screen few people reach: measure exposure first.
