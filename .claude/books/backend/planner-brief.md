plan-trip puts every matched place on a day its people are near it, close to the day's other plans, on a day it is open, around bookings, with a rough time and one short line. No AI: hours and facts come with the card (`place_library` to `candidates`); a card without them is planned from its kind and `time_needed`.

**Files** (`supabase/functions/plan-trip/`)
- `index.ts`: auth, reads tables, writes `itinerary_items` and `places.auto_planned`.
- `lease.ts` (`loop`; branch planner-fixes-2026-11-26): one planner per trip, and what the app reads of a run in `plan_state`. Taking the lease clears `error`; a failed run writes `error` as code and time (`plan_failed:<ISO time>`), so the app's check `row.error !== before.error` (src/App.tsx) also sees the same failure twice in a row. `plan_state` has no started column.
- `errors.ts` (`failBody`, `failMark`; same branch): a failed call answers `{ error: 'plan_failed' | 'stays_failed', message }` with status 500; the error's text and stack go to `console.error` only. Test: `node scripts/plan-error-test.mjs`.
- `presence.ts` (`buildDays`): who is where each day (stays, bookings, couples' own days). On the branch `planner-fixes-2026-11-26` (not in main, not deployed): a city-change day starts after the booked train or from check-out plus the ride, the departure day counts the ride to its airport, a couple with a baby ends by 20:30. Date-only tickets are a known weak spot (qa).
- Ids and the search (round 10, branch `planner-fixes-2026-11-26`, not deployed). Four changes in `plan.ts`:
  - Order: `plan()` sorts the places with `inOrder()`: what more couples liked first, then more stars, then the shorter visit, then everything else the place is (city, kind, point, hours, flags); the id only between two places alike in all of that. `mealSlots()` and the out-and-back candidates break their ties the same way. So the plan no longer changes with the ids (test `r21`: ten random relabels of the made-up 20-day trip give the same places, days and times; the round-9 code gave 95 to 97 placed there).
  - Late places: `mendLate()` in `arrange()` mends a day whose timetable reaches a place after it closes: a stop whose leaving puts the day on time moves to a day that takes it cleanly, else the cheapest one waits. `solve()` still bars and re-runs when a day stays late, but that is now rare (about 9 to 11 `arrange()` runs a plan, from 21 to 30).
  - Out-and-back: a fourth step when move, swap and wait all fail because a meal would leave its slot: now the meal may give way (fewest meals lost, then least wanted). No day keeps a ride back.
  - Lift (after the free hours): a place left out is tried on each of its days with one stop taken off; the stop goes to another day that takes it, or waits when the place left out comes before it (what both couples liked at 4 stars or more comes before everything that is not; otherwise more stars and no fewer couples). For a place both couples liked at 4+ the stop taken off may itself take another stop's room once, and when no single stop makes the room, two or three lower stops come off together. A day's lunch and dinner and day trips stay. It runs in every `arrange()`, also when the plan as it stands is kept, so a new swipe can now move or take off a stop that was planned.
  - What this did NOT do: the search is no steadier than it was. With the ties broken at random (12 draws) the real trip gives 81 to 84 placed (round 9: 81 to 84) and the what-if 93 to 97 (round 9: 93 to 100); the order above is one fixed draw from that spread, and on the real trip it is a good one (85 placed). Multi-start (2 to 4 tie orders, best taken) did not narrow the spread and cost 150 to 400 ms; deeper lifts cost CPU for under one place. Still open: a place both couples liked at 4+ can stay out when only other such places hold its days (real trip 1, what-if 3 that fit in a plan of those places alone); that needs the shared 4+ places laid out first and the rest added around them, not a repair at the end.
- Tiers (round 11, same branch): tried and NOT kept; `plan.ts` is as round 10 left it. The try: the places both couples liked at 4 stars or more (every 4+ place in a one-party trip) laid out alone first, then the full plan built around them with those places free to change day inside their city but never to wait. Measured with the processing order drawn at random (40 draws; round 10 as it is: real trip 81 to 85 placed, mean 82.7, 31 to 33 meals; what-if 91 to 97, mean 94.5, 36 to 39 meals):
  - Shared 4+ held: all 46 that fit alone are placed (round 10: 45 real, 43 what-if), but the real trip falls to 79 to 83 placed and 30 to 31 meals, the what-if to 88 to 93 and 34 to 37; on the committed order 83 placed / 30 meals (from 85 / 33) and 90 / 34 (from 96 / 38). Each such place takes both couples' hours on a day that is already full (Kyoto, Osaka), so a couple's own places and own meals leave.
  - Meals in the first tier as well: about as round 10 (real 81 to 85, what-if 92 to 98), meals lower on the committed order (32 and 36), and the shared 4+ places no better kept (44 to 45).
  - A deeper lift for every place (two steps): mean up 0.3 to 0.4 of a place, the lowest draw the same, the committed real trip down to 84 / 32 meals. Three more from-nothing starts in other orders, best taken: no gain, a third more CPU.
  - So the two asks pull against each other on these trips: every shared 4+ place in costs meals and places; the planner's own measure (`worthOf`) rates the two plans within a few percent. The spread under random order is still there; the committed order is one of its best draws. A general check "what fits in a plan of the shared 4+ alone is in the full plan" does not hold (real 1, what-if 3; fixtures `real-japan.json` 1 and `trip-20-days.json` 4, those four day trips), so no test asserts it.
  - Tests added for two round-10 steps: `r22` (the lift: a mall both liked at 4 stars is not left out for a couple's own 3-star market) and `r23` (the late mend: the stop the plan loses least by gives way, not the late place; a place that is open is not called closed). Each fails with its step switched off. The out-and-back last step (a meal gives way) has no test: it fired on a random-order draw of the what-if, never on the committed order of either input or of the 20-day fixture, and on no small made-up day (about 20,000 tried).
- `parties.ts`: who a trip is planned for. A party is a couple (`members.couple`). A trip where no member has a couple (every trip the app creates) is ONE party of all its members: every place of the trip is liked (a place is in `places` only after a match; `plan_place_couples` answers such a trip with `couples: null` and no votes), every stay, day, flight and train is theirs whatever `who` a row carries, the pace is the calmest member's, a baby of one member is everyone's. The party's inner name (`*`) is never written to a row or sent to the app: no `couple` on its issues, `summary.days[].hotels` and `.arrival` keyed `''`, `stay_areas[].party` null. A mixed trip (some members with a couple, some with none) has the named couples as its parties, and a member with no couple is one more voice that goes with the whole group (round 14): their like counts as a yes for the place, their no or silence takes nothing off and holds nothing up, and they have no days or stays of their own. Their voice reaches the planner as `'~' || user_id` after the couples in `voted`/`yes` of `plan_place_couples` (`LONE`, `isLone`, `likedFor` in `parties.ts`); it is never written to a row or sent to the app. This needs `supabase/sql/2026-11-27-lone-members-vote.sql` (written, NOT applied; it patches the live `plan_place_couples` in place and is safe to run twice); until it is applied such a member's swipes are not counted, as before. Not changed: which places join a trip. `match_place`, `couple_vote`, `couple_both` and `trip_place_couples` still count couples only, so a place only such a member liked is not added; and their pace and baby answers are not read. On the branch `planner-fixes-2026-11-26`, not deployed. Test: `npm run test:plan-parties` (index.ts itself, dry, on made-up trips: one, two and three people with no couples, one named couple, a couple and a party of one, a mixed trip, two couples and a member with no couple, where-to-sleep). `scripts/plan-fixtures/four-couples.json` is a made-up trip of four couples for `npm run test:plan`.
- `geo.ts` (`locate`, `addressVariants`; same branch): address to coordinates through `geo_cache` only (free, cached; a miss is asked again after 14 days). `areaPoint` (round 3): a card with no coordinates whose address is not found gets the main station of the neighbourhood its card (or address) names, from `_shared/areas.ts`; an area that names no place stays unknown.
- `plan.ts` (`plan`, `hhmm`, `km`): the day packer. `stay.ts` (`baseFor`, `sleepsFromStays`): the hotel base point per night. `split.ts` (`suggestSplit`, `openNights`): "suggest a split" of open nights between cities, reads `cities` (route, ideal nights, season).
- `flags.ts` (`servesDinner`, `daylightOnly`; same branch, round 2): the two flags a card gets, from its facts (category, best time, visit minutes, hours, plan line), never from words in its name. A nightlife card is a dinner only when it says izakaya or yakitori, asks for the evening and takes an hour; a sight is held to daylight only when it has no hours and asks for the daytime, or (round 4) has no hours, says "any" and its name or plan line calls it a path, a trail, a walk, a garden or a park. Cards carry no "outdoors" fact: a flag that can't be known is left off.
- Client side: `replan` in `src/lib.ts` asks for a re-plan; the Plan tab opens call it too.

**Rules**
- A plan item a person added, moved or edited is never touched; only `auto` items are. Bookings and expenses must never change (planner fixes ship before 12 Dec only after replay passes, memory notes).
- Triggers call it after each swipe, match, booking, stay change; `request_plan()` sets `plan_state.dirty` and only starts the function when no planner runs.
- Tests: `npm run test:plan` (fixtures in `scripts/plan-fixtures/`), `npm run test:plan-fixes` (one test per fix of 2026-11-26, and `r1` to `r7` for the round-2 fixes of 2026-11-27: shared places first, flags from facts, a lone far place, meals on a day in another city, an early dinner, known points, a late lunch; `r8` to `r12` for round 3: a neighbourhood's station as a place's point, an unknown point first or last in the day, a couple that flies out in the morning is not company, free hours take a place left out (a meal counts 45 minutes of pace there, checked against the day's times), a shared morning market as the lunch of the couple that has none; `r13` to `r15` for round 4: a walk in the open with no hours and no time of day is over by dusk, a day that starts by 14:15 gets a late lunch first (started by 14:45; a later start has no lunch to plan), one couple's own far place between two stops in the same part of town moves to another day or waits (no out-and-back); `r16` for round 5: any stop of an out-and-back moves (the place of the fewest people first), one couple's own place or a place of a couple alone that day waits when no day takes it, a place everyone on a shared day liked only moves (round 6 changed this: see `r17`); `r17` to `r19` for round 6: no day keeps an out-and-back even when every stop on it is shared (timed again with the ride back costing more than a missed best hour, then move, then swap with a stop of another day, then the fewest, least wanted wait; a lunch or dinner never goes for it), a needed meal slot takes a liked meal place of that city before a non-meal stop keeps its room (up to two stops give way), a couple's day with no meal gets one from a day of theirs in the same city that has two, a lunch pushed out of its hours comes back into them; `r20` for round 9: the same places in another order give the same plan (the made-up 20-day trip as written, backwards and in five shuffles); `r21` for round 10: the same places under other ids give the same plan (ten random relabels of that trip); before/after numbers with `node scripts/plan-metrics.mjs`), `npm run test:split`, `npm run test:stay`, `node scripts/rank-test.mjs`. For a bug write the failing fixture first.
- CPU (round 7, 2026-11-27): an edge function gets about 2 s of CPU for one request, and the planner had grown to 5.8 s on a 139-place trip. `npm run test:plan-cpu` fails when a 139-place, 20-day fixture goes over 1200 ms (new process each time, best of three; about 1100 ms after round 7, about 790 ms after round 12). What keeps it fast in `plan.ts`: one `memo` for the whole run, shared by every solve; each ordered line of places on a day has a number (`listId`, `joined`, `left`), and a day's load, meals, cost and fits are remembered by that number; a day's timetable search (`walkOrder`, `improveOrder`) works on index tables and drops an order as soon as it costs more than the best. The plan must come out the same byte for byte after a speed change: same sums in the same order, same order of places in a day's set. Check with a replay before and after. Whether Supabase counts the compile threads' time is not known (main thread alone: about 850 ms on that trip, whole process: about 1350 ms).
- CPU and cost (round 12, 2026-11-27; same branch, not deployed). In `plan.ts`, same plan byte for byte: a day's order that begins like the one being improved is walked from where the two part (`walkOrder` keeps where the walk stood after each step: `ck`, `from`, `keep`); a change of two neighbours, or of the ends of a stretch of three, is tried once instead of three times; `improveOrder` stops when a whole round of tries has passed since its last gain, and the second start stops when it reaches the order the first one settled on; a walk checks only fixed hours for a clash (its own earlier stops always end before the next can start). `memo.ts` (`planOnce`, `inputKey`): the last input of each trip (up to 6 trips) and its plan are kept in the function's memory; a run whose input is the same to the letter does not plan again, it still compares the plan with the rows and writes what differs (nothing). Only while the function stays loaded; a dry run always plans. Test: `npm run test:plan-skip`. Round 13 (same day, same branch, not deployed): a run reads `geo_cache` in one read for every address it may look up (`cacheKeys`, `primed` in `geo.ts`; `.in('q', keys)`, split when the list is long, and a key the read could not carry or a failed read falls back to one read for that key); it was one read for each way of writing each address (53 on the real trip's replay, 98 on the what-if, now 1). The order of the lookups, the 8 network lookups a run and their pause are as they were. `lease.ts` clears `dirty` after the 2 s wait, so a request inside the wait is covered by the run that follows and no longer costs a second round (a change is saved together with its mark, so one saved after the clear marks again). Written, not applied: `supabase/sql/2026-11-27-replan-on-read-fields.sql` (see triggers). Not done: the hash of the input is not kept in the database, so a cold function plans again.
- Real trip stays untouched: prove with fixtures, never run the planner on it (dry run needs the planner token, not for agents).

Known open (qa): hotelBase picks Kanagawa before Hakone; some Tokyo wards missing (couple's days dropped); undated trip says "updated" but does nothing; same-coordinate places.
