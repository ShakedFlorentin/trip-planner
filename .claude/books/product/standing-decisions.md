1. English with every UI change; check the mirrored layout.
2. No per-user AI or paid per-call APIs. Plain code, saved data or cache.
3. A place enters a trip only by swipe and match.
4. Weak cards are left out (no description, or no address or neighbourhood). Report how many.
5. Photos must look good, or the card keeps its icon tile.
6. No risky deploys before the Japan trip ends (Dec 2026). Where-to-stay and planner changes wait; small fixes may go file by file.
7. Money: affiliate hotel search is the main income; credits cover AI cost plus a little; no squeezing.
8. Design reviews list every problem, with before/after images.
9. Git is the owner's. Agents write files and report.
10. Issue-box reports: the owner's own may be fixed; others need approval.
11. Never run the app with a real account; only the "Toogo Test" simulator or mocks.
