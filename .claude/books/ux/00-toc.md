Read this first. Who it is for: the ux agent (design reviews, copy, flows) and anyone touching how Toogo looks and reads. Standing rules: `.claude/skills/toogo-rules/SKILL.md` (1 English with every change, 8 list every problem and show a before/after image). Skills to link, not copy: `toogo-premium-feel`, `toogo-first-run`, `toogo-ux-ui-craft` (rules filled by the teacher agent over time), `toogo-ux-audit` (five-line brief and audit table for every review), `ui-sdk`. When a chapter and a skill disagree, the skill wins and the chapter says so in one line. Decision history: `docs/design-decisions.md`, `docs/design-review.md`, `docs/review-2026-11-14-ux-and-qa.md`.

- [01-tokens-and-type](01-tokens-and-type.md): colour, type ramp, Hebrew tracking, radii, spacing, motion, dark mode (src/styles.css).
- [02-layout-patterns](02-layout-patterns.md): sheets, rows, cards, chips, tab bar, timeline, buttons and their six states.
- [03-rtl-and-ltr](03-rtl-and-ltr.md): Hebrew first, English mirror, what flips and what does not.
- [04-states-empty-loading-error](04-states-empty-loading-error.md): empty / loading / offline / error / no permission, and where errors go.
- [05-motion-and-haptics](05-motion-and-haptics.md): springs, the key moments, timing, reduced motion, haptics (built).
- [06-accessibility](06-accessibility.md): names, focus, targets, contrast, text size, with the known gaps.
- [07-copy-voice](07-copy-voice.md): plural "we", Hebrew first, what/why/action errors, wording by stakes.
- [08-flow-first-run-and-add](08-flow-first-run-and-add.md): first run, add place, swipe, match, plan.
- [09-flow-bookings-money-share](09-flow-bookings-money-share.md): add booking, add expense and settle, credits, share.
- [10-ux-debts](10-ux-debts.md): open UX problems: the 2026-11-16 QA round and the 2026-11-27 audit against creators' rules, by severity.
- [11-learned-from-creators](11-learned-from-creators.md): one-page map from each rule learned from reels to its chapter, Toogo screens and skill.
