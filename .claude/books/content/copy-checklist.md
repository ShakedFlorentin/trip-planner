---
title: Copy checklist before handing back
chapter: process
---
1. Restate what a reader will see (for a bug-driven fix: the externally observable behaviour, not the ticket words).
2. Hebrew first, in the owner's voice; plural "we".
3. English written fresh, same meaning, same change (rule 1).
4. Mirrored layout checked (icons, arrows) and text fits in both languages.
5. No promise of a feature that is not live (rule 6); no fake urgency (rule 7); no "kosher".
6. Facts checked against the app or the docs; counts ("1,500 places", "237 cards") quoted from a current source, with the date.
7. Legal-weight lines match [legal-copy-cautions](legal-copy-cautions.md); placeholders left visible.
8. Store text within limits (name 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100 bytes, promo 170); table with character counts.
9. Files touched named; app strings handed to frontend, not edited in `src/` by content.
10. Re-read every changed file; check related copy for conflicts.
11. Report which skill was used and what was not checked.

Related: [voice-and-copy-rules](voice-and-copy-rules.md).
