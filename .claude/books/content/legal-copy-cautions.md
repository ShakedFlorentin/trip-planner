Content writes the words; legal advises and a human lawyer decides. Drafts: `docs/legal-drafts-2026-11-14.md` (nothing is live).

## Do not write
- "It never includes what you type": error events can carry up to 200 characters of screen text.
- "Anthropic keeps it only for a limited time": unless Anthropic's current terms are cited.
- "May earn us a commission": there are no partner IDs yet. Use "Partner links will be marked as such".
- "Kosher" or any certification claim. "No pork or seafood" is not kosher certification.
- "Everything is deleted" for account deletion: a shared trip keeps what the leaver added and their display name next to expenses; shared library places stay with no link to them.
- "Nothing is shared": share links show place names, kind, location, city per day, days and short tips (can be off).
- Photo wording: "forever" / "for good" is a licence; do not change without legal.
- "We store data in Frankfurt": region is [VERIFY].

## Must say
- Who runs it and a contact (placeholders until the owner decides).
- Which providers receive data: Supabase, Anthropic (US; booking files may hold passport numbers), OpenStreetMap Nominatim, GitHub Pages.
- That AI suggestions can be wrong; check hours and bookings.
- The split of expenses is a calculator, not money transfer.
- A Terms of Use link in the store description (rejection risk).

## Placeholders
Keep `[OWNER NAME]`, `[CONTACT EMAIL]`, `[LIABILITY CAP]`, `[COURT]`, `[VERIFY REGION]` visible until the owner or lawyer fills them. Never invent them.
