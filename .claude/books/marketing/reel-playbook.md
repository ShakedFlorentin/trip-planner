## Hook shape
Another person + a conflict -> I showed them -> they changed their mind. Never open with the app's name or a feature. Write the hook in Hebrew first.
- "היא רצתה חוף, אני רציתי מוזיאונים. שנינו עשינו לייק על זה."
- "ארבעה חברים, טיול אחד, אפס ריבים בקבוצה."

## Formats
- Photo slideshow, 6 portrait slides: hook, plan unfolding (places, match, day), soft call to action. Cheapest. Slides can be built from the cards table by script, a person picks photos that pass rule 5.
- Reel: question hook, friends' own footage, short app demo on the beat. Post the same reel again with new audio and cover.
- Demo must show swipe and match (rule 3), made-up data (rule 11).

- Text on video: six words max per screen, held about 1.2 s. A demo is not a screen recording: crop to one flow, a finger leads. Pick the format by viewer stage (curious, understand, decide); one of each.
- One ask per caption; give the value in the caption, never behind "comment a word".

## Rhythm
TikTok first; Instagram did worse for small apps. Long flat start is normal. Judge a hook after 3-5 posts. Text on video: Hebrew is longer, check limits separately.

## Deliverable
Per idea: hook (HE and EN), the 6 slides or shot list, caption, the Toogo screen shown.

## Do not
Phone farms, bots, fake accounts, promising features not live, paid influencers before organic works, posting for the owner.
