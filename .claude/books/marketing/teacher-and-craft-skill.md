The `teacher` agent reads creators' reels and teaches four craft skills. Marketing, growth and content load `toogo-marketing-craft`.

## What goes into toogo-marketing-craft
Positioning, brand, reels and hooks, launch, store listing, growth loops, pricing ideas. Each rule is short, with a one-line why and the reel link. Narrow topics live in `toogo-short-video`, `toogo-store-listing`, `toogo-growth-loops`, `toogo-indie-launch`, with a one-line pointer in the craft skill.

## State on 16 Nov
`.claude/skills/toogo-marketing-craft/SKILL.md` had its Rules section empty ("Filled by the teacher agent") when this book was written. Until it is filled, use the narrow skills above.

## How to use
Read the skill before a brief. Rules pass `toogo-rules` first: no squeezing, Hebrew plus English, swipe and match, photos, nothing risky before the trip.

## Asking for more
Send the owner's reel or channel link to Atlas; Atlas dispatches the teacher. See the teacher book (`reel-learning-loop`).
