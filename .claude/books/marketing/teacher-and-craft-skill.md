The `teacher` agent reads creators' reels and folds the lessons into craft skills. Marketing, growth and content load `toogo-marketing-craft`.

## What goes into toogo-marketing-craft
Positioning, brand, reels and hooks, store listing, launch, growth loops, pricing ideas, landing page. Each rule is short, with a one-line why and the reel link. Narrow topics live in `toogo-short-video`, `toogo-store-listing`, `toogo-growth-loops`, `toogo-indie-launch`, `toogo-credits-offer`, `toogo-app-review`, with a pointer in the craft skill.

## State on 27 Nov
The skill is filled. It was re-read in full on 26-27 Nov from the signed-in creator re-reads (@ceciliakimdesign, @nels_ysng, @danh.app, @kunaluxui, @synsation_, @aniakubow). The 16 Nov note that its Rules section was empty is out of date; the skill wins.

## How to use
Read the skill before a brief. Rules pass `toogo-rules` first: no squeezing, Hebrew plus English, swipe and match, photos, nothing risky before the trip. Creators' numbers are their claims; the evidence is thin.

## Asking for more
Send the owner's reel or channel link to Atlas; Atlas dispatches the teacher. See the teacher book (`reel-learning-loop`).
