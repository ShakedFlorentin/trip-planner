Full draft: `docs/app-store-listing.md` (13 Nov). Rules: `toogo-store-listing`. Store files go to `docs/appstore/`. The appstore book owns review readiness; this page is about selling.

## Fields (limits)
Name 30 chars, subtitle 30, keywords 100 characters per `toogo-store-listing` (this page said bytes; the skill wins, confirm the Hebrew count in App Store Connect), promo text 170. No word repeated across name, subtitle, keywords; commas without spaces.

## Drafted
- EN: "Toogo: Japan Trip Planner" / "Plan Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka".
- HE (Israel store): "Toogo: תכנון טיול ליפן" / "מסלול יומי: טוקיו קיוטו אוסקה". Hebrew search matches whole words, so prefixed words (ליפן) do not match יפן: put יפן in keywords.
- Second locale idea: English (UK) for Israel to add keywords (a dated 2022 trick, verify).
- Category: Travel; secondary Food and Drink.

## Screenshots
5 strong beat 10; first two carry the decision. Order (skill: swipe, match, day plan, map, split costs; the draft's nights per city, bookings, food or baby are alternates, keep to 5). Real UI, short headline each, Hebrew set mirrored plus English set, only good photos. **Do not show where-to-stay** (the draft list has it at #5: drop it until live).

## Description cautions
Says "no pork or seafood", never "kosher". Includes a Terms of Use link. "1,500 places" must match what is live. Mentions offline and share extension only when on.

## Brand folder and listing text (`toogo-marketing-craft`, `toogo-store-listing`)
Check icon, name, subtitle, screenshots, preview video, share card and reviewer notes side by side, in Hebrew and English. One benefit per screenshot, app in use (not logo, login or splash), one accent on the one thing. Say plainly what the app does; a stranger should say the one line from subtitle alone. Terms of use link in the description. Study two competitor listings first.

## Testing
Product Page Optimization after approval, one change per test.
