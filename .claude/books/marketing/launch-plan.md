Nothing risky before the Japan trip ends (rule 6). No budget; the owner's own voice is the channel.

## Now to 12 Dec (prepare, do not publish risky things)
- Write the first 3-5 hooks and slideshows in Hebrew and English; build slides from real cards with good photos.
- Draft the store listing; keep screenshots free of where-to-stay.
- Capture real screens on the "Toogo Test" simulator with made-up data.

## The trip (12-31 Dec)
The owner's own trip is the real test. Collect honest footage the owner chooses to share. Do not post for the owner.

## After the trip (January)
1. Live free search pages from the library cards; list on directories (AlternativeTo, SaaSHub). These give affiliate programmes the website they ask for.
2. Delete account, privacy contacts, demo account, then App Store submission and listing per country.
3. Turn on where-to-stay with the hotel link; apply to Booking.com and Klook.
4. First video series (TikTok first). Judge hooks after 3-5 posts.
5. Rating prompt after a match or first day plan (Apple's own).
6. Open sign-up (guest mode, Google plus Apple) per the growth book.

## Timing notes
December is crowded for everyone, and launch is after the trip anyway. Release the app by hand on a day the owner can watch; no expedited review: the trip is not an accepted event. A tester round capped for a true reason may say so once.

## Metric
One per step: installs from a source (`open` by source), first swipe, first match.
